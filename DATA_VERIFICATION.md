

## Supplied audit follow-up (22 November 2026)

Reviewed both supplied classification CSVs (194 rows and 25 rows; overlapping samples).
Compared each supplied reason against the current classifier. New explicit bone bruise,
plural knees, mouth, illness, family/personal and maternity terms are recognized.
Medical exemption/protocol/return-to-play, surgery without a specific cause and generic
rehab remain unknown with a health-related flag. Dental surgery does not establish
traumatic injury; cyst surgery does not establish illness. Generic load management
remains other; an explicitly named knee injury remains injury-related source wording.

Completed box scores with fewer than expected team minutes minus 30 (200 regulation,
25 per overtime), or a starter explicitly marked DNP, receive a source-review flag.
All three overview averages exclude flagged games. The original player rows, source
absence counts, charts and downloadable observations remain source-reported; the
flag and reason accompany game and player exports. A flag does not establish the
correct participation or prove an injury. The threshold deliberately tolerates rounded
minutes. This is an automated sanity check, not independent game-book verification.

ESPN comparisons were not supplied, so the claimed 3,325-game audit was not reproduced.

samples are not equivalent. Unmatched official rows remain unlinked without evidence.
