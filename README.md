# WNBA
WNBA Injury Data Repo

Season availability and historical limits (2026-11-22)

The season selector now uses `data/season_coverage.json`. An availability audit checked 930 schedules: 1997–2027 × 15 currently selectable franchise IDs × regular season/playoffs. For each nonempty schedule, the first completed player box score was sampled. This is not an all-games injury audit. Seasons 1997–2002 and 2027 had no usable sampled player data and are hidden; 2013–2026 remain selectable. The catalog does not cover every defunct historical franchise. Adding future years requires verifying source data and refreshing the coverage manifest, so an empty future season is not automatically advertised.

The overview always shows two separate sources with fixed meanings. “Recorded reasons for not playing” counts ESPN-listed absences by source reason. “Pregame injury report listings” counts official WNBA PDF designations, including players who may still play. Missing PDFs display one “No official PDF data for this selection” message, not four blank totals. Zero means no matching records in a loaded source, not proof of a complete injury-free history. The sources must not be added together. The table headers, player detail, CSV buttons and downloaded filenames name their source; ESPN injury absences and WNBA PDF injury listings have separate exports. Duplicate lower injury summaries were removed.

Historical ESPN reasons remain incomplete. Atlanta 2025 first20 contains only two ESPN injury-DNP rows; the official May25 gamebook lists injured inactive players omitted from ESPN. Historical injuries have NOT been comprehensively backfilled. Example: https://statsdmz.nba.com/pdfs/20250525/20250525_CONATL.pdf

Latest source comparison

Open “Compare latest source listings” and load ESPN/Action Network. `/api/current-injuries` retrieves their independent source lists with a five-minute cache, dates, attribution, original statuses/details and warnings on rows over seven days old. The official WNBA source remains dated game snapshots; no latest-page snapshot is claimed. Outlet lists may include offseason-stale statuses, disagreements, duplicates and non-injury reasons. They are **not joined to historical games, counted as unique injuries, or treated as primary confirmation**. Failed retrieval is Unavailable. The comparison export includes source provenance, confidence and the requested research fields; unreconciled season, side, actual return date and games missed remain blank. A source body-site label is preserved only when explicit; no diagnosis is inferred.

Evidence hierarchy: team/league statements first; credible outlet reports remain reported pending primary verification; encyclopedia pages are leads only. Official CSV confidence “confirmed” confirms the league report designation, not a diagnosis, incident or actual absence. Box-score and news rows are “reported.” Game-log gaps never establish injury. Games missed and actual return dates require dated injury evidence plus game-by-game reconciliation. Overseas/offseason origin remains unverified unless sourced. Pre-2023 minor injuries and short absences may be substantially under-covered. No complete public injury registry or clinical validation is claimed.

Final selector criterion: usable player data plus an identifiable injury reason in the availability sample. 2003–2012 have some player data but no identifiable injury reasons in these samples, so they are also hidden pending injury verification. Visible seasons: 2013–2026. Sampling does not prove the hidden years have no public injury information, nor that visible years are complete.

Follow-up data review on 22 November 2026

The source-text classifier now recognizes additional ligament, muscle and spine terminology and named musculoskeletal terms such as plantar fasciitis, tendinitis and bursitis. It does not treat every `-itis` word as injury. Explicit ambiguous health wording, including unqualified Injury/Illness, health and safety protocols, collapsed lung and reconditioning, remains Unknown with a visible **Health-related, cause unknown** subtotal. This subtotal is included in Unknown and must not be added to it. Generic rest/load management/injury management remains Other unless a specific injury, body-site or illness reason is reported; no new injury is inferred from load management alone.

Official PDF names now normalize hyphen spacing in both full and source names and preserve the extracted spelling in `source_player_name_raw`. Full source-provided name variants can match only when unique; no fuzzy or surname-only matching is performed. Unmatched rows remain unverified rather than receiving guessed IDs or zero minutes. Player/report CSVs include the health subtotal flag, classification note, source/raw names, unmatched reason, pregame-Out/played review flag, UTC game start and PDF game date.

The UI explicitly counts missing player box scores. Refresh source bypasses cached ESPN data, and failed HTTP requests are not cached. A pregame Out designation followed by ESPN participation is flagged for review, preserving both source values and the selected snapshot timestamp. This may reflect later updates or a source discrepancy; it is not automatically treated as a bad PDF or injury absence.
