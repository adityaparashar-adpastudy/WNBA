Interpretation limits:
These checks validate extraction, joins and aggregation against selected public
source responses. They do not independently medically adjudicate every fact.
Snapshots are not necessarily the final pre-tipoff revision, a complete archive
of all public injuries, or unique injury episodes. Injured players may still play.
Raw ESPN minutes are usually rounded and are not verified exact-second exposure.
Source access, corrections and report layouts can change after this check.
No zero-error, complete-registry or clinical-validation certification is made.
