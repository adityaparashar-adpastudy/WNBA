MEASURING WHAT WE CAN OBSERVE

Know the definitions.

This dashboard adapts the supplied NBA Flask dashboards and WNBA ESPN scrapers into a single WNBA workflow.

Box-score listed
Unique players listed for the team in that game’s ESPN box score. This is a game-specific count, not a season roster size or proof that every player was dressed and available.
Entered / player-game appearances
A player has a numeric minutes value and is not flagged as did-not-play. A numeric zero can represent rounded sub-minute participation.
Reported DNP categories
Each non-participant is assigned one source-text category: injury (explicit injury or body-site reason), illness (explicit illness), other (recognized non-injury reasons or participation statuses), or unknown (missing, ambiguous, or unrecognized reason). Injury and illness counts are separate. Mixed “INJURY/ILLNESS”, “COLLAPSED LUNG”, and reconditioning reports stay unknown and are marked as health-related; the source does not establish a cause. These are player-game counts, not unique injuries, diagnoses, or new injury events. Original source wording is preserved in Player detail. Other does not prove the player was healthy or available.
Game dates
Dates use the actual venue’s IANA timezone, including neutral-site venues and daylight-saving changes. If the venue is unrecognized, the UTC date is explicitly labeled. CSV exports preserve the date timezone, date basis, original UTC date, and UTC start timestamp.
In-game injuries
Not available from these box scores. No in-game injury rate is calculated.
Minutes and overtime
Regulation is 40 elapsed minutes × 5 players = 200 team player-minutes. Each overtime adds 25 player-minutes. OT count comes from the game’s period field; unavailable period data appears as a dash. Summed source minutes can vary due to rounding.
Coverage and data source
Completed games only, ordered chronologically. Commissioner’s Cup qualifying games are included; the separately flagged Cup championship is excluded. A sample is explicitly labeled; failed games are excluded from all averages and listed in the status. No fixed season length is assumed. The public ESPN JSON endpoint is undocumented and may change. Schedules refresh after five minutes and the team catalog after 24 hours. Box scores are cached locally; use Refresh source to fetch again.
